The Lantern Archive

Chapter 12: A Map of Lost Stars

Fantasy · AdventureChapter 124 min read

Mira reached the gates of the Lantern Archive just as the last bell faded. Beside her, Rowan held a lantern against the rain. Somewhere beyond the silver doors, the Star Council was waiting.

“The Cartographer left us a warning,” Rowan said. He unfolded the map on the stone steps. Every road led toward the Glass Harbor, but one thin line continued into the sea.

Mira recognized the mark at once: the Ashen Crown. She had seen it on a letter from Elian, the quiet scholar who had vanished three nights before. Now she understood why he had trusted the Cartographer.

Inside the Lantern Archive, a thousand lamps burned without a flame. Elian stood beneath the tallest shelf, a jade pendant resting in his palm. “The Star Council cannot read this map,” he whispered. “Only you can.”

Rowan glanced back toward the Glass Harbor. “Then we leave before sunrise.” Mira closed her hand around the pendant. For the first time, the road ahead felt like a promise instead of a threat.
